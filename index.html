Hi, I’m Zimo Chen

I am a PhD candidate in Design at Tongji University, with research interests at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction and Intelligent Design. My research focuses on developing multimodal interface interaction systems and psychophysiological computing models for autonomous unmanned transportation scenarios, aiming to improve communication efficiency, interaction safety, and affective adaptability among humans, vehicles, and the environment.
